Behind the song

What kindness
leaves behind

I wrote “One More Angel” to honor Dolly Parton and the kindness, generosity, and love she’s shown others.

Her example got me thinking about what it means to give somebody hope. Kindness can start with noticing when someone needs a hand and doing what you can to help.

People shared the song after I posted it on social media. Seeing it find an audience there encouraged me to release it as Sawyer Silas. I hope the song brings to mind someone who’s been that kind of person for you.
