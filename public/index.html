Behind the featured single

When the river
came alive

The river looked like it was glowing from within.

Mayflies lifted off the water, their wings catching the light. Trout rose to meet them, and rings spread across the surface. It felt like the whole river had come alive.

About the artist

Sawyer Silas

Sawyer Silas makes country music with an outlaw-country edge and a touch of blues. “Caught in the Rise” came from a fly-fishing trip to West Virginia and the feeling of watching a river come alive.
